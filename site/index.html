Source-grounded by design

Recall without surrender.

Local Data Finder is an index, not a new home for your information. Point it at the places you choose. It gives every match back with the path and extraction time needed to verify it.

  1. 01

    Choose the boundaries

    Add only specific folders or explicit exports. Hidden folders and unsupported files are skipped.

  2. 02

    Build a local evidence map

    Text is extracted on-device from Markdown, plain text, HTML, mbox and text-based PDFs. Mail attachments stay closed.

  3. 03

    Search, inspect, open

    Filter exact lexical matches, read the surrounding evidence, then open the original local source.
