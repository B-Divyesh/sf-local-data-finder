Privacy

Local Data Finder processes selected records on your device.

Desktop app

Selected paths, extracted text, search queries, and index settings stay on your device. The app does not send your selected files, paths, or search queries to a service. Opening a source uses your operating system.

Website

The site uses no advertising, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or analytics SDK. Standard short-lived server logs may contain an IP address, requested path, user agent, and timestamp for security and reliability.

Your control

Remove a source to remove its extracted records from the index. This does not delete the original files. The sample project uses a separate local index and is discarded when you start for real.

Contact

Privacy questions can be filed in the public project repository without including personal archive content.
