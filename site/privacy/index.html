Privacy

Local Data Finder processes selected records on your device.

Desktop app

Selected paths, extracted text, search queries, and index settings stay on your device. The app does not send your selected files, paths, or search queries to a service. Opening a source uses your operating system.

Website

The static site loads no advertising, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or analytics SDKs.

Your control

Remove a source to remove its extracted records from the index. This does not delete the original files. The desktop sample uses a separate local index and is discarded when you start for real. The browser demo stores its query only under a demo key and clears it when you start for real.

Contact

Privacy questions can be filed in the public project repository without including personal archive content.
